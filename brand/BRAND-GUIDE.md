# Aromatabs — brand guide

**Aromatabs** is an authority on rest and relaxation. The brand has to feel
calm without feeling weightless, and credible without feeling clinical. Every
decision below serves that pair.

---

## The name and the wordmark

The wordmark is **aromatabs**, always lowercase, always Source Serif Pro
Semibold with +8/1000em tracking. Lowercase keeps it quiet; the serif keeps it
serious. Never set it in another face, never capitalise it, never letterspace
it further.

In running prose the brand is written **Aromatabs**. Only the wordmark is
lowercase.

## The mark

A tab dissolving into still water: one drop above three spreading ripples,
inside a soft-cornered tab.

It reads as diffusion — which is what an aroma tab does — and as the settling
that follows. It is legible down to 16px, which is why the ripples are three
and not five.

Two forms:

- **Tab** — the drop and ripples knocked out of a filled squircle. The default.
- **Glyph** — the drop and ripples alone, one colour. For monochrome print,
  embroidery, favicon pin, and anywhere the tab would fight the background.

## Lockups

| Lockup | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Horizontal | Site header, email, anything wider than it is tall |
| Lockup (with tagline) | Covers, banners, first impressions |
| Stacked | Square and portrait formats, merchandise, print |
| Wordmark | Where the mark already appears nearby |
| Mark | Avatars, favicons, app icons, watermarks |

Four colourways: **color** (light grounds), **reverse** (dark grounds),
**mono-ink**, **mono-white**.

**Clear space** is half the mark's height on every side. Nothing enters it.

**Minimum sizes** — horizontal lockup 160px / 40mm wide; stacked lockup
120px / 32mm wide; mark alone 20px / 6mm.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | `#0F1E1C` | Body copy, headlines on light |
| deep | `#10352F` | Dark sections, logo ground |
| eucalyptus | `#2E7D6F` | Primary brand, links, buttons |
| eucalyptus-600 | `#24675B` | Hover, small text on light |
| eucalyptus-300 | `#7FB3A5` | Text on dark, muted labels |
| mist | `#CFE2DA` | Dividers, cards on dark |
| linen | `#F3EEE4` | Page background |
| cloud | `#FDFBF7` | Surfaces, cards on light |
| ember | `#C98B45` | Accent, CTAs, highlights |
| ember-300 | `#E8C79B` | Accent on dark, pills |

The greens carry the brand; **ember is a seasoning**, not a second brand
colour. Used on roughly one element per screen it reads as warmth. Used on
three it reads as a different company.

Backgrounds are warm (linen, cloud), never cold grey. That warmth is most of
what separates Aromatabs from clinical sleep brands.

Three gradients, and no others: **mark** (eucalyptus → deep), **dark plate**
(deep → ink, diagonal), **reverse mark** (cloud → mist).

## Typography

**Source Serif Pro** for anything with a voice — headlines, ledes, pull
quotes, the wordmark. **Source Sans Pro** for everything else — body copy on
the site, UI, labels, captions, data.

Both are open source (SIL OFL 1.1) and self-hosted. Nothing loads from a
third-party font CDN.

Labels and eyebrows are uppercase Source Sans Semibold at +160 to +240/1000em
tracking. That tracking is a brand signature — keep it consistent.

## The ripple motif

Concentric rings, blown far past the edge of the canvas, at 12–20% opacity.
They give every surface a family resemblance without adding a single image.

Rules: the origin sits off-canvas or near an edge; rings never sit behind body
copy at a contrast that costs legibility; and the motif never appears inside
article content, where it competes with the words.

## Voice

Plain, warm, specific, unhurried. Short sentences. Second person.

We say *"the wind-down window"*, not *"your sleep journey"*. We say *"we don't
know yet"* when the evidence is thin. No hype, no exclamation marks, no
promises about tomorrow morning.

Because the subject is health, the voice carries an obligation: every claim is
cited, every citation is real and linked, and nothing on the site is medical
advice.

## Photography and illustration

Prefer none. Where an image is genuinely needed: low light, warm shadow, real
rooms, no models performing sleep, no lavender-on-white stock. The mark's
ripple motif carries the visual identity so that pages can stay text-first.
