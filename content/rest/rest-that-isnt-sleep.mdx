We should be upfront: this is the thinnest evidence base on the site. "Rest" as
a category is hard to define, harder to measure, and does not attract the
funding that sleep does. What follows is a reasonable frame, not an established
science.

## Why sleep alone is not enough

The recovery literature keeps arriving at the same distinction: what predicts
recovery is not time off but psychological detachment — actually being
elsewhere.[^sonnentag2015b] Sleep supplies that unavoidably. Nothing else does
automatically.

So a person can sleep eight hours a night and still be in a state of continuous
low-grade engagement for the other sixteen. That person is not rested, and more
sleep is not the fix.

## The kinds worth having

**Undirected attention.** Time when nothing is asking anything of you and your
mind is free to wander. Walking without a podcast. Staring out of a train
window. This is the kind most thoroughly displaced by phones, and the one people
most often report missing without being able to name.

**Time outdoors.** The evidence here is better than for most of this article,
though still modest. In one study, twenty to thirty minutes of unstructured time
in a natural setting was associated with a measurable drop in salivary cortisol,
with diminishing returns beyond that.[^hunter2019] Small sample, self-selected
setting, and worth treating as suggestive.

**Solitude.** Distinct from loneliness, and for people whose work is
continuously social, the thing that is actually depleted.

**Absorption in something with no stake.** An activity that occupies attention
completely and does not matter. The stakelessness is the active ingredient — a
hobby you have turned into a second job is not rest.

## The thing to notice

Almost none of these feels productive, which is why they are the first to go and
why people substitute sleep for them. Sleep at least has a health rationale you
can defend.

If you are sleeping adequately and still feel depleted, adding an hour to the
night is unlikely to help. The missing thing is probably in the sixteen hours,
not the eight.
